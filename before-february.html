You have done more than anyone can check.

Not a criticism of the work. A description of the paper trail. Every figure attached to your programmes counts what went out. Almost nothing counts what came back.

54% of Nandi has not decided. They will choose the candidate who can show them something, and right now nobody in this race is showing anything.

06On the attacks

The story is smaller than it looks.

We read fourteen Nandi Facebook groups from the inside and had the Kalenjin translated. Searching oripoo in the 50,000-member Nandi Hills forum returns three posts — one of them supportive.

The rejection post carries 3 reactions and 35 shares. That is content being pushed, not spreading.

The cultural line is specific and checkable, not a slur — which means it can be answered with facts. The reason it ran at all is that nobody holds your original footage. That is the structural fix, not a statement.

07The question coming

Somebody has already written the hard question.

Bring the numbers. Bring the beneficiary lists. Bring the procurement records. Circulating in the Nandi Hills groups

No gender, no culture, no insults — which is exactly why it is dangerous. It also asks the one thing nobody has answered: if the trees mature and there is no factory or buyer, what then?

Better to answer it first, in our own words, with our own evidence.

08Build one

The film

A payment slip with a woman's name on it.

A 24-minute documentary on Kahawa na Mama. Not a profile — a record. Trees planted in 2023 are bearing now, and that three-year before-and-after can be filmed this season and never again.

The programme's real innovation is not the seedlings. It is the charter putting a wife's name beside her husband's on a coffee bush. Almost nobody knows it exists.

Screened across the six sub-counties in January, in village halls before the internet.

11The open ground

Tea

The biggest crop in Nandi. Nobody has claimed it.

Coffee is yours. But tea is where most of Nandi's money is, and no aspirant in this race has taken a position on bonus rates, factory financing, or who controls the payment.

You already have the method. Kahawa na Mama started by asking women what they didn't control about a cash crop.

Run that same diagnostic at tea. Listening forums in the tea wards, and publish what farmers actually say. Not a manifesto promise from a podium — a record of the question being asked, which is the thing nobody else is doing.

Research first, position second. Tea politics is genuinely contested and arriving with the wrong answer is worse than arriving with the question.

12Already yours

The set-piece is already in your calendar.

Thirty wards. 499 teams. 846 matches. Built in three months. That is not a sports programme, it is an organising network with named people in every ward of Nandi.

The county grand finale falls in December 2026 — weeks before nominations. It is funded, it is running, and nothing currently treats it as the moment it is.
