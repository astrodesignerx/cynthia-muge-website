05
First build

The film

A payment slip with a woman's name on it.

A documentary on Kahawa na Mama. Not a profile of you — a record of what the programme did. Trees planted in 2023 are bearing right now, and that three-year change can be filmed this season and never again.

Camera, sound, interviews, edit, subtitles, grade. I do all of it myself.

Screened across the six sub-counties in January, in halls, before it ever goes online.

06
Second build

The rhythm

Something true, every week, until nominations.

One short video a week, each ending on a single fact with its source on screen. Plus same-day clips from every public appearance, so your words travel with your context attached rather than someone else's edit.

Captioned, always. That alone is the difference between 80,500 views and 802.

Filming, editing, captioning, scheduling and publishing — handled, so your job stays the part you are best at: speaking to people.

09
The ask

What I would need from you.

  • Access — to your diary, your programme records and the people who ran them
  • An introduction to four or five farmers, and to the cooperative
  • Permission to film freely at public events, including the ones that go badly
  • A decision that the honest numbers get published, even where they are lower than we hoped
  • One person in your office who owns the day-to-day publishing with me

I am not asking to run your campaign. I am asking to make your record visible — starting with the film, because the trees are bearing now and that will not wait.
