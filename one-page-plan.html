01 Confirm the coffee harvest windowOne call to Baraka Agricultural College or the county AFA office. The whole filming schedule follows this date. This week
02 Caption every postFree, immediate. The measured difference on the same account is 80,500 views against 802. This week
03 Pin the drifting figuresScholarships appear as both 409 and 449; the target as 3,500, 4,000 and 120 per ward. Fix one set and use it everywhere. This week
04 Count the surviving treesA 90% survival rate is circulating from a supporter’s estimate. Measure it before anyone prints it — and before the film is committed to. Sept
05 Start owning the footageFilm every public appearance in full, publish same day. This is the structural answer to the August controversy. Sept
06 Film the harvestTrees planted in 2023 are bearing now. This window does not come back. Oct – Dec

One decision to make early

That the measured numbers get published even where they are lower than hoped. Agreed in September this costs nothing; agreed in December it costs an argument.

What I do

Seven capabilities, each attached to something on the left. All of it executed personally unless noted.

Film and photography The documentary end to end, event coverage, farmer and beneficiary interviews, the stills archive. Items 05 · 06 · 08
Sound and audiovisual Location recording, interview audio, mix and score. Clean sound is what separates a film from footage. Items 06 · 08
Graphic design and identity One mark, one type system, one set of templates, so anything published is recognisable at a glance. Items 02 · 07
Digital and publishing Captions, scheduling, the weekly rhythm, same-day turnaround from event to post. Items 02 · 05
Web and application development The evidence site and the database behind it, built so the office updates it without me. Item 07
Data visualisation County budget explainers, programme charts, the graphics that answer a false figure with a sourced one. Items 03 · 07
Research and verification Source-checking, monitoring what is being said, and the tracking system that keeps every claim traceable. Items 01 · 03 · 04
Not mine — bring in specialists Polling needs a licensed research firm. Election law, campaign finance and the cooperative branding question need a lawyer. Ward organising and agent recruitment are their own discipline. Someone must own overall strategy and message discipline; I make the evidence, I should not also decide how it is spent. Counsel on the cultural questions needs someone with standing in Nandi.
